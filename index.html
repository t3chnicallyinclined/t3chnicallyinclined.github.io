How I work

Start from what changed. Most breaks follow a change — a renewal, a rotation, a deploy, an edited config. Evidence and documentation to an assumption, then confirm it before anyone acts.
Cut scope until it can be tested. Two platforms I can test this week beat five I can't.
Check what already exists before building it again. In our own stack first, then outside it.
Do the cheap reversible thing first. The expensive safeguard waits until the evidence asks for it.
Use real inputs, and change one thing at a time.
